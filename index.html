Chapter IV. Method

Four phases. No drift.

Phase 0114 days

Discover

Two-week audit of current systems, ownership, and pain. Findings delivered as a written brief — not a deck.

Phase 0210 days

Architect

Technical design reviewed with your team. Scope, risk, and acceptance criteria locked before any code ships.

Phase 036—14 weeks

Build

Delivery in two-week increments. Weekly demos, visible metrics, a shared backlog auditable at any moment.

Phase 0430 days

Transition

Runbooks, training, a 30-day handover to your internal team. We leave when you are ready — not when the budget runs out.
